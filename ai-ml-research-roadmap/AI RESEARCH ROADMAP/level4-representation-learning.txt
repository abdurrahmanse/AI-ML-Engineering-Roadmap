Level 4 · Representation Learning & Latent Metric Spaces

Scope: examine how deep neural networks learn semantically rich representations without human labels: contrastive learning, self-supervised objectives, metric learning, the manifold hypothesis, and dense vector spaces.

Prerequisites: Level 3

M4.1 · The Manifold Hypothesis & Self-Supervised Objectives — (sessions: 3)

Understand the Manifold Hypothesis: real-world high-dimensional data (images, text, audio) concentrates on lower-dimensional non-linear sub-manifolds.
Study linear dimensionality reduction: Principal Component Analysis (PCA) derivation via maximum variance and minimum reconstruction error, probabilistic PCA.
Analyze non-linear dimensionality reduction: t-SNE (t-Distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding, perplexity, Student-t distribution) and UMAP (Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection, Riemannian geometry basics).
Master self-supervised pretext tasks: autoencoding, masked language modeling (BERT), masked image modeling (MAE, He et al.), and autoregressive prediction as representation learners.
Analyze the reconstruction versus contrastive objective trade-off: pixel-level reconstruction wasting capacity on high-frequency details vs semantic feature extraction.
Outcome: derive PCA analytically using Rayleigh quotients and implement a Masked Autoencoder (MAE) baseline that reconstructs masked image patches.

M4.2 · Contrastive Learning Theory & InfoNCE — (sessions: 4)

Master Contrastive Representation Learning: pulling positive pairs together while pushing negative pairs apart in a metric embedding space.
Derive the InfoNCE loss function (Oord et al., CPC): connection to Noise-Contrastive Estimation (NCE) and mutual information maximization lower bound.
Analyze SimCLR (Chen et al.): composition of data augmentations, projection head non-linearity, temperature parameter tau, and large batch size dynamics.
Understand Representation Collapse: the trivial constant-representation failure mode in contrastive learning and mechanisms to prevent it.
Study Momentum Contrast (MoCo, He et al.): decoupling dictionary size from batch size using a dynamic memory queue and momentum-updated target encoder.
Investigate Non-Contrastive Self-Supervision: BYOL (Bootstrap Your Own Latent, Grill et al.) and SimSiam (Chen & He) avoiding collapse without negative pairs using stop-gradient operators.
Analyze Barlow Twins (Zbontar et al.) and VICReg (Bardes et al.): information-theoretic objectives enforcing invariance, variance preservation, and cross-correlation decorrelation.
Outcome: prove mathematically that InfoNCE loss optimizes a variational lower bound on mutual information between views, and implement SimCLR with InfoNCE loss in PyTorch.

M4.3 · Metric Learning, Deep Embeddings & Triplet Geometries — (sessions: 3)

Understand Metric Learning: learning distance metrics d(x_i, x_j) such that semantically similar items have small distances while dissimilar items have large distances.
Master Contrastive Loss (Hadsell et al.) and Triplet Loss (Schroff et al., FaceNet): anchor, positive, and negative geometries, and margin hyperparameter alpha.
Analyze Negative Mining Strategies: offline vs online negative mining, hard negative mining, semi-hard negative mining, and easy negative saturation.
Explore Angular and Cosine Margin Losses: SphereFace, CosFace, and ArcFace (Additive Angular Margin Loss, Deng et al.) for discriminative feature spaces.
Understand embedding normalization: why projecting representations onto the unit hypersphere S^(d-1) stabilizes cosine distance metrics and temperature scaling.
Study properties of representations on hyperspheres: Wang & Isola's alignment (closeness of positive pairs) and uniformity (uniform distribution over the hypersphere) metrics.
Outcome: implement ArcFace with angular margin in PyTorch and compute Wang & Isola alignment and uniformity scores for learned representations.

M4.4 · Dense Vector Retrieval & Semantic Search Science — (sessions: 4)

Formulate the information retrieval task in dense representation spaces: bi-encoder architectures, cross-encoder rerankers, and dual-encoder scaling.
Analyze dual-encoder training: in-batch negatives, hard negative mining (BM25 negatives vs dense retrieval negatives), and multi-stage distillation (ColBERT, late interaction).
Understand the geometry of high-dimensional vector spaces: orthogonality of random vectors, concentration of distances, and hubness problem.
Analyze Exact Nearest Neighbor (k-NN) search complexity and the theoretical limits of sub-linear retrieval in high dimensions.
Study Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) algorithms: graph-based indexing (Hierarchical Navigable Small World, HNSW), inverted file indexes (IVF), and product quantization (PQ).
Evaluate dense retrieval quality using ranking metrics: Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR@k), Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain (NDCG@k), and Recall@k.
Outcome: implement a bi-encoder dense retriever with hard negative mining, index embeddings using HNSW, and measure NDCG@10 on a search benchmark.

M4.5 · Probing & Analyzing Latent Representations — (sessions: 3)

Understand Representation Probing: testing what linguistic or perceptual information is linearly decodable from intermediate neural activations (Alain & Ollivier).
Master Linear Probing: training frozen linear classifiers on intermediate representations and interpreting probe accuracy as information presence.
Analyze the limitations and caveats of linear probes: differentiating between information extraction versus computation, and probe complexity trade-offs (Hewitt & Liang control tasks).
Study Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA, Kornblith et al.): comparing representations across different architectures, layers, and random initializations invariantly to orthogonal rotations.
Analyze Intrinsic Dimension Estimation: Two-NN algorithm, maximum likelihood estimators, and tracking how intrinsic dimension changes across deep network layers.
Examine Anisotropy in Language Model Embeddings: representation degeneration, singular value decay, and embedding cluster geometry in hyperspherical spaces.
Outcome: implement Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) from mathematical scratch and compare the layer-wise representational similarity between two trained models.

M4.6 · Capstone: Self-Supervised Representation Reproduction and Latent Geometry Probing — (sessions: 2)

Reproduce a seminal self-supervised representation learning method (SimCLR or Masked Autoencoder) on an unlabelled scientific image or text dataset.
Implement the full data augmentation pipeline, encoder network, non-linear projection head, and InfoNCE / reconstruction objective.
Track empirical alignment and uniformity metrics over the unit hypersphere throughout training to monitor representation geometry.
Evaluate learned representations using standard linear probing protocol: freeze the encoder backbone and train a linear classification probe on extracted representations.
Implement Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) to visualize cross-layer representational similarity and observe where abstract representations emerge.
Conduct an ablation study comparing: (1) with vs without non-linear projection head, (2) temperature scaling variations in InfoNCE, and (3) hard negative mining vs random negatives.
Evaluate out-of-distribution transfer performance of the learned representations against a fully supervised baseline.
Write a scientific research report presenting t-SNE / UMAP latent visualizations, CKA heatmaps, alignment-uniformity scatter plots, and linear probe accuracy curves.
Final result: a reproducible self-supervised representation codebase and research paper evaluating latent space geometry and downstream transfer capabilities.

Level 4 completion outcome

By the end of this level, you should be able to:

Explain the Manifold Hypothesis and formulate representation learning in terms of low-dimensional manifolds.
Derive the analytical formulation of Principal Component Analysis (PCA) using constrained optimization.
Explain the mathematical objective of t-SNE and its use of the Student-t distribution to resolve crowding.
Derive the InfoNCE loss function and prove its connection to mutual information maximization.
Explain the representation collapse problem in self-supervised learning and how BYOL and SimSiam avoid it.
Derive the Triplet Loss and compare hard negative mining and semi-hard negative mining strategies.
Implement ArcFace (Additive Angular Margin Loss) and explain why angular margins encourage intra-class compactness.
Quantify representation quality on the unit hypersphere using Wang & Isola's alignment and uniformity metrics.
Contrast bi-encoder and cross-encoder architectures in terms of accuracy, inference latency, and indexing feasibility.
Explain how Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW) graphs achieve approximate logarithmic nearest neighbor search.
Compute standard ranking evaluation metrics including MRR@k and NDCG@k from scratch.
Train linear probes on frozen neural network representations and evaluate probe task performance.
Implement Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) and construct cross-model representation similarity matrices.
Identify anisotropy in transformer representations and explain why word embeddings tend to collapse into narrow cones.
Progression: Manifold Hypothesis & Dimensionality Reduction → Contrastive Learning & InfoNCE → Metric Learning & Hyperspherical Geometries → Dense Vector Retrieval → Representation Probing & CKA → Self-Supervised Reproduction Capstone.
