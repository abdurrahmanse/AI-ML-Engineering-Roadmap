Level 7 · Generative Modeling & Probabilistic Machine Learning

Phase: Generative Models & Benchmarking
Suggested duration: 13 sessions (~3 weeks)
Prerequisites: Level 6 (Sequence Models, Graph Neural Networks & Transformers)
Scope: Master probabilistic and generative models for data synthesis, density estimation, and anomaly detection: Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM), score-based generative modeling, and Continuous Flow Matching (CFM); generating privacy-preserving synthetic clinical cohorts (MIMIC-IV) and synthetic cybersecurity attack vectors; evaluating generative fidelity and diversity via Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), Precision & Recall for Generative Models, and membership inference vulnerability.

M7.1 · Latent Variable Models & Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) — (sessions: 2)
Session 1 · The Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO) & Reparameterization
- Formulate continuous latent variable models: the intractable marginal likelihood p(x) and variational posterior approximation q(z|x).
- Derive the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO) from Jensen's inequality and KL divergence, separating reconstruction loss from latent prior regularization.
- Implement the reparameterization trick (z = mu + sigma * epsilon) allowing low-variance reverse-mode gradient backpropagation through stochastic nodes.
Session 2 · Disentangled Latents & Tabular Patient Generation
- Analyze beta-VAE: balancing reconstruction fidelity against latent factor disentanglement and addressing catastrophic posterior collapse.
- Implement Conditional VAEs (C-VAE) conditioned on clinical diagnostic labels or cybersecurity network attack categories.
- Generate synthetic mixed-type tabular clinical records (continuous vitals + discrete diagnosis codes) using categorical Gumbel-Softmax relaxations.
Mental model: The reparameterization trick isolates the stochastic sampling into an external noise parameter, enabling end-to-end gradient flow.
Try: Implement a Conditional VAE in PyTorch, train it on mixed-type tabular records, and generate synthetic samples conditioned on specific disease labels.
Outcome: Derive the ELBO, implement the reparameterization trick, and generate conditioned tabular samples using Variational Autoencoders.

M7.2 · Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM & Score Matching) — (sessions: 2)
Session 1 · Forward Diffusion & Reverse Denoising
- Formalize the forward discrete-time Markovian diffusion process: gradually corrupting clean data x_0 into isotropic Gaussian noise via a variance schedule beta_t.
- Derive the closed-form sampling equation for arbitrary timestep t without iterative stepping (x_t as a linear combination of x_0 and standard Gaussian noise).
- Train a reverse denoising U-Net predicting the injected noise epsilon_theta under a simplified mean-squared error objective (Ho et al.).
Session 2 · Score-Based Generative Models & Continuous SDEs
- Connect DDPM to score-based generative modeling (Song & Ermon): learning the gradient of the log probability density (the Stein score function) at multiple noise scales.
- Formulate diffusion as continuous Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs): Variance Preserving (VP) and Variance Exploding (VE) SDEs.
- Sample from reverse SDEs and Probability Flow ODEs using Euler-Maruyama numerical solvers and predictor-corrector algorithms.
Mental model: Diffusion reverses entropy; by training a neural network to predict noise at every scale, we can trace a trajectory from pure noise back to the data manifold.
Try: Train a 2D Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model (DDPM) on a radiological imaging dataset in PyTorch, implementing the full reverse sampling loop.
Outcome: Master the mathematics of discrete and continuous diffusion, train denoising backbones, and generate high-fidelity samples from pure noise.

M7.3 · Continuous Normalizing Flows & Optimal Transport Flow Matching — (sessions: 2)
Session 1 · Neural Ordinary Differential Equations & Continuous Flows
- Master continuous-time invertible models: mapping base Gaussian densities to complex target data distributions via Neural ODEs (Chen et al.).
- Compute exact log-likelihoods using the instantaneous change-of-variables formula and Hutchinson's trace estimator for scalable Jacobian trace computation.
- Contrast the straight-line paths of continuous flows against the curved, stochastic trajectories of Brownian motion in standard diffusion.
Session 2 · Flow Matching & Optimal Transport (OT-CFM)
- Implement Flow Matching (Lipman et al.): regressing time-dependent vector fields directly without costly numerical ODE simulation during training.
- Apply Optimal Transport Conditional Flow Matching (OT-CFM): establishing straight-line probability paths between noise distributions and data points.
- Benchmark inference speed: generating high-fidelity samples in as few as 10-20 deterministic Euler steps, drastically outperforming standard DDPM sampling.
Mental model: Flow matching straightens the generative trajectories into straight lines, enabling dramatically faster deterministic sampling with fewer ODE solver steps.
Try: Implement an Optimal Transport Flow Matching model in PyTorch on a 2D synthetic dataset, visualize the learned velocity vector fields, and integrate trajectories.
Outcome: Formulate continuous normalizing flows and implement Optimal Transport Flow Matching for fast, deterministic generative sampling.

M7.4 · Domain-Specific Generative Synthesis: Medical Scans & Cyber Telemetry — (sessions: 2)
Session 1 · Medical Image Synthesis & Lesion Inpainting
- Apply conditioned latent diffusion models to generate high-resolution synthetic chest radiographs (CheXpert) conditioned on diagnostic text prompts.
- Synthesize realistic rare pathological lesions (e.g., glioblastoma brain tumors on MRI) to augment severely data-scarce medical training splits.
- Perform lesion inpainting and counterfactual image generation: modifying specific anatomical regions while preserving patient-specific identity features.
Session 2 · Cybersecurity Attack Synthesis & Traffic Obfuscation
- Generate realistic, synthetic bidirectional network flow telemetry mimicking stealthy advanced persistent threat (APT) multi-stage attack campaigns.
- Synthesize functional polymorphic malware feature vectors that mimic benign Windows PE executables to test intrusion detection evasion.
- Ensure format-preserving constraints: enforcing protocol validities, packet size limits, and non-negative integer counts in synthetic network traces.
Mental model: Generative models can solve extreme domain data scarcity by synthesizing high-fidelity, physically consistent rare pathological lesions and cyber threats.
Try: Train a conditioned diffusion model to synthesize realistic rare pathology samples, and verify that training a downstream classifier with augmented data improves AUROC.
Outcome: Deploy state-of-the-art diffusion and flow models to synthesize high-utility medical images and cybersecurity telemetry.

M7.5 · Generative Evaluation Metrics, Fidelity & Privacy Auditing — (sessions: 2)
Session 1 · Sample Quality & Diversity Metrics (FID, Precision & Recall)
- Compute Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) to quantify distributional distance between real and synthetic feature embeddings extracted from pre-trained backbones.
- Decompose generative performance into Precision (fidelity of generated samples) and Recall (diversity and coverage of the target distribution) (Sajjadi et al.).
- Evaluate domain-specific realism: clinical radiological feature preservation evaluated by domain experts, and packet header validity checks for cyber telemetry.
Session 2 · Privacy Auditing & Membership Inference Vulnerability
- Understand the privacy risks of synthetic data: generative models can memorize and regurgitate individual training records, violating HIPAA or GDPR.
- Execute Membership Inference Attacks (MIA): training shadow models to determine whether a specific patient record was present in the generative training set.
- Quantify attribute disclosure risks and evaluate Differentially Private Diffusion (DP-Diffusion) using Rényi Differential Privacy accounting.
Mental model: High visual fidelity is meaningless if the generative model simply memorized and reproduced confidential patient scans; privacy auditing is mandatory.
Try: Calculate FID, Precision, and Recall on synthetic samples, and implement a shadow-model membership inference attack to verify that training patient data is not leaked.
Outcome: Evaluate generative fidelity, diversity, and distributional realism, and conduct empirical privacy audits to prevent training data memorization.

M7.6 · Capstone Project: Domain-Specific Generative Diffusion & Privacy Synthesis Framework — (sessions: 3)
Session 1 · Generative Pipeline Architecture & Conditioned Training
- Build an end-to-end generative synthesis pipeline using either Denoising Diffusion (DDPM) or Optimal Transport Flow Matching (OT-CFM).
- Target a specialized domain problem: synthesizing privacy-preserving patient cohorts (MIMIC-IV clinical events) or realistic cyber attack flows (CIC-IDS).
- Implement label conditioning (Classifier-Free Guidance) enabling targeted generation of rare minority classes (rare pathologies or stealthy cyber intrusions).
Session 2 · Empirical Evaluation, Downstream Utility & Privacy Verification
- Benchmark generative quality: compute FID / Wasserstein distance, Precision, and Recall against real validation splits.
- Evaluate downstream empirical utility: train a predictive classifier exclusively on synthetic data and test on real unseen patient/network test sets (TSTR: Train on Synthetic, Test on Real).
- Execute a membership inference attack audit to guarantee that synthetic records do not leak private training data under differential privacy standards.
Mental model: The ultimate test of synthetic data research is downstream clinical or security utility combined with mathematical privacy guarantees.
Try: Run the full synthesis pipeline, train a downstream model on synthetic data, evaluate on real test data, and produce a publication-ready evaluation report.
Outcome: Deliver an end-to-end, audited generative synthesis framework with proven downstream utility and verified privacy guarantees.
