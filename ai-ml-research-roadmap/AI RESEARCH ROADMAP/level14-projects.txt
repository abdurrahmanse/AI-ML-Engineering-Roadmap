Level 14 · Comprehensive Research Capstones

Phase: Frontier, Alignment & Thesis
Suggested duration: 25 sessions (~6 weeks)
Prerequisites: Level 4 (Prototyping), Level 7 (RL & Search), Level 8 (Benchmarks), Level 9 (HPC), Level 11 (Interpretability), Level 12 (Alignment)
Scope: Execute seven end-to-end, conference-grade research projects embodying the complete lifecycle of MSc/PhD dissertation chapters and flagship submissions to premier AI/ML venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ACL).

P14.1 · Flagship Project 1: Rigorous Reproduction of a Top-Tier Conference Paper — (sessions: 3)
Select a seminal, highly cited paper published within the last 24 months in a top-tier machine learning conference (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR).
Re-implement the core model architecture, custom loss functions, and data preprocessing pipelines from scratch in PyTorch without copying official source code.
Execute the full experimental training suite under strictly identical hyperparameter budgets, hardware settings, and random seeds.
Identify unstated implementation tricks, missing mathematical assumptions, or discrepancies between reported paper metrics and empirical reproduction results.
Perform an ablation study evaluating the sensitivity of the reproduced method to learning rate schedules, seed variation, and batch size scaling.
Author a formal scientific reproduction report adhering to the NeurIPS Reproducibility Challenge guidelines, complete with verified code release.
Outcome: A scientifically verified reproduction codebase and comprehensive artifact report validating or disputing published claims.

P14.2 · Flagship Project 2: Novel Sequence Architecture with Empirical Scaling Analysis — (sessions: 4)
Design and implement an innovative sequence modeling architecture uniting sub-quadratic State Space Models (Mamba) with local self-attention mechanisms.
Derive the analytical time and memory complexity of the proposed hybrid architecture, proving linear or near-linear scaling with sequence length.
Pre-train a family of models across multiple compute budgets (10M to 250M parameters) on standardized multi-domain text corpora on an HPC cluster.
Fit empirical Chinchilla scaling laws to quantify compute-optimal parameter and token allocations for the proposed hybrid architecture.
Evaluate associative recall and long-context reasoning using synthetic needle-in-a-haystack and real-world long-context document understanding benchmarks.
Profile hardware execution: measure Model FLOPs Utilization (MFU), peak VRAM consumption, and wall-clock inference latency up to 64k tokens.
Outcome: A novel, fully benchmarked sequence model architecture accompanied by empirical scaling curves and compute efficiency analyses.

P14.3 · Flagship Project 3: Decontaminated Domain Benchmark with Human Agreement Calibration — (sessions: 4)
Curate a novel, multi-task domain-specific evaluation benchmark dataset comprising at least 500 challenging multi-step reasoning instances.
Author a rigorous annotation codebook, conduct multi-annotator human evaluation, and verify statistical reliability (Krippendorff’s alpha > 0.80).
Build an automated 13-gram contamination detection scanner using suffix arrays to verify zero overlap with standard open-source pre-training corpora.
Evaluate a comprehensive suite of proprietary and open-source foundation models under standardized zero-shot and few-shot prompting conditions.
Implement a bidirectional LLM-as-a-Judge pipeline, calibrating automated scores against human expert consensus via Kendall’s tau and Spearman rank correlation.
Format all dataset artifacts with Datasheets for Datasets documentation and release clean evaluation harnesses on Hugging Face Datasets.
Outcome: A pristine, decontaminated domain benchmark established as an open-source evaluation standard for the research community.

P14.4 · Flagship Project 4: Mechanistic Circuit Investigation & Steering in Frontier LLMs — (sessions: 3)
Select a complex, non-trivial capability in an open-weight foundation model (e.g., indirect object identification, counterfactual reasoning, or sycophancy).
Apply causal activation patching across all layers and token positions to map the exact minimal computational circuit mediating the target capability.
Deconstruct mediating attention heads into mathematically formulated QK addressing circuits and OV information-routing circuits.
Train an overcomplete Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) with L1 regularization on the critical bottleneck layer, extracting monosemantic feature dictionaries.
Extract contrastive activation steering vectors and demonstrate targeted behavioral steering during generation without degrading general language perplexity.
Author an interactive research report featuring causal circuit wiring diagrams, activation heatmaps, and mechanistic ablation proofs.
Outcome: An end-to-end mechanistic interpretability discovery identifying the internal computational circuit and demonstrating precise latent steering.

P14.5 · Flagship Project 5: Continuous Flow Matching Diffusion Model for Scientific Modalities — (sessions: 4)
Formulate and implement a continuous-time Optimal Transport Conditional Flow Matching (OT-CFM) generative model on a complex scientific modality.
Implement a modern Diffusion Transformer (DiT) velocity backbone with adaptive layer normalization conditioning and classifier-free guidance.
Construct straight-line probability paths between standard Gaussian priors and complex empirical data manifolds.
Implement accelerated adaptive ODE numerical solvers (Runge-Kutta 4, midpoint) achieving high-fidelity generation in 15 to 25 steps.
Evaluate generative distribution quality rigorously using clean Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), Precision, Recall, and trajectory curvature metrics.
Benchmark generation speed, kinetic energy dissipation, and sample diversity against classical DDPM and score-based SDE baselines.
Outcome: A state-of-the-art continuous flow matching framework with publication-quality distribution metrics and accelerated numerical sampling.

P14.6 · Flagship Project 6: Preference Optimization Regularization with Spectral Analysis — (sessions: 3)
Formulate a novel regularization objective for Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) that penalizes rank collapse in the weight update matrix Delta W.
Implement the spectral regularization algorithm in PyTorch, applying low-rank Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) constraints during preference tuning.
Train a foundation model on challenging conversational preference datasets, monitoring implicit reward margins and policy KL divergence.
Audit downstream performance: evaluate retention of mathematical reasoning, coding abilities, and factual truthfulness, quantifying the alignment tax.
Perform singular value spectral decomposition on weight deltas to verify that foundational pre-trained feature representations remain uncorrupted.
Conduct red-teaming audits to prove enhanced resistance to reward hacking, sycophancy, and adversarial jailbreak perturbations.
Outcome: A novel preference optimization methodology published with mathematical derivations, empirical proofs, and spectral analysis.

P14.7 · Flagship Project 7: End-to-End Conference Submission: Paper, Codebase & Data Release — (sessions: 4)
Synthesize empirical discoveries from one of the preceding flagship projects into a full 9-page conference paper manuscript in LaTeX (NeurIPS/ICML format).
Draft all required paper sections: Abstract, Introduction, Related Work Taxonomy, Mathematical Methodology, Experimental Setup, Results, Ablations, and Limitations.
Design publication-quality vector figures (using TikZ, Matplotlib/Seaborn science themes, and Illustrator) depicting architecture, scaling, and circuits.
Package the complete research codebase into a public GitHub repository with Dockerized environment manifests, pre-trained weights, and automated tests.
Draft an exhaustive reproducibility checklist detailing compute resources, random seeds, hyperparameter search grids, and negative findings.
Simulate a formal conference submission package ready for submission to OpenReview with anonymized review-ready artifacts.
Outcome: A complete, publication-grade academic conference submission package ready for submission to a premier peer-reviewed AI venue.
