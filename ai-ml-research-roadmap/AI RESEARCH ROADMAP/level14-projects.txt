Level 14 · Research Projects & Scientific Reproduction

Scope: rigorous empirical research artifacts that compose earlier theoretical and practical levels. Execute conference-grade research experiments, reproducing seminal results, testing novel algorithmic hypotheses, and producing publication-quality scientific reports.
Prerequisites: Level 3 (Transformers), Level 6 (Reasoning), Level 8 (Alignment), Level 11 (Diffusion), Level 12 (Multimodal)

P14.1 · Self-Supervised Representation Reproduction on Scientific Domains — (sessions: 3)

Implement and reproduce a seminal self-supervised learning architecture (SimCLR or Masked Autoencoder) on an unlabelled scientific benchmark.
Construct a rigorous data augmentation pipeline tailored to the domain, verifying invariance properties.
Execute linear probing evaluations on extracted representations, tracking validation classification accuracy against fully supervised baselines.
Perform Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) and t-SNE / UMAP latent space projections to probe representation geometry across network depth.
Outcome: a verified reproduction repository and empirical ablation report demonstrating high-fidelity latent representations without supervision.

P14.2 · Architectural Benchmark: Sub-Quadratic State Space vs Attention Hybrid — (sessions: 3)

Design, implement, and benchmark a hybrid architecture combining local self-attention with Mamba selective state space layers.
Evaluate long-context associative recall on needle-in-a-haystack synthetic probes and standard language modeling benchmarks.
Profile wall-clock inference latency, KV cache memory footprint, and training throughput (TFLOPs/s) across sequence lengths from 2K to 64K tokens.
Analyze the trade-offs between linear state space efficiency and multi-head attention associative recall accuracy.
Outcome: a clean PyTorch implementation and empirical benchmark paper characterizing the Pareto frontier of hybrid sequence models.

P14.3 · Scalable Synthetic Data Engine with Step-Level Symbolic Verification — (sessions: 3)

Build an automated synthetic data curation engine that generates complex multi-step reasoning traces paired with automated symbolic verification.
Implement execution-based verifiers (Python interpreters and formal logic solvers) to validate intermediate reasoning steps deterministically.
Fine-tune a smaller student foundation model on the curated synthetic dataset, measuring task accuracy gains while monitoring for model collapse.
Conduct ablation studies on data filtering thresholds, generation diversity parameters, and verification pass rates.
Outcome: an end-to-end synthetic dataset generation pipeline and fine-tuned student checkpoint demonstrating empirical reasoning improvements.

P14.4 · Mechanistic Dissection of In-Context Learning Circuits — (sessions: 2)

Apply mechanistic interpretability techniques to locate, isolate, and analyze induction heads and attention circuits in an open-weight foundation model.
Perform activation patching and path patching across attention layers to identify the exact causal circuit responsible for multi-token in-context retrieval.
Extract concept steering vectors and test whether intervening on specific attention head outputs modulates target reasoning capabilities.
Train a Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) on intermediate MLP activations to isolate monosemantic features associated with the identified circuit.
Outcome: an interactive mechanistic interpretability notebook and circuit diagram report detailing the causal mechanics of in-context retrieval.

P14.5 · Continuous Optimal Transport Flow Matching on Complex Manifolds — (sessions: 3)

Train a continuous-time Flow Matching generative model with optimal transport displacement paths on scientific dynamical systems or image datasets.
Implement adaptive layer normalization conditioning and a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) velocity backbone.
Compare ODE integration trajectories and sample quality against standard Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) across 4, 10, and 50 sampling steps.
Evaluate generative fidelity using Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) and trajectory kinetic energy integrals.
Outcome: a high-performance flow matching training and sampling codebase with empirical benchmarks confirming straight-line velocity trajectories.

P14.6 · Robust Preference Optimization with Spectral SVD Regularization — (sessions: 2)

Implement Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) augmented with low-rank singular value decomposition (SVD) constraints on weight update matrices.
Evaluate alignment performance on conversational preference benchmarks (MT-Bench, AlpacaEval) while tracking general-domain benchmark retention.
Perform spectral analysis of weight update matrices Delta W to confirm preservation of pre-trained feature subspaces during preference optimization.
Measure resistance to reward hacking and sycophancy compared to standard unconstrained DPO and PPO baselines.
Outcome: a custom alignment training script and research report evaluating spectral constraints in preference optimization.

P14.7 · Research Paper Draft, Code Release & Reproducibility Suite — (sessions: 2)

Format empirical research findings into a complete, conference-ready scientific paper using the standard LaTeX template (NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR).
Structure the paper following best academic practices: Introduction, Related Work, Methodological Formulation, Experimental Setup, Results & Discussion, and Limitations.
Package the codebase into a clean, public GitHub repository with Dockerized environment configs, pinned dependencies, model weights, and reproduction scripts.
Include detailed documentation of compute resources, random seeds, hyperparameters, negative results, and failure modes to ensure gold-standard reproducibility.
Outcome: a polished, peer-review-ready academic paper draft and public reproducibility repository ready for conference submission.
