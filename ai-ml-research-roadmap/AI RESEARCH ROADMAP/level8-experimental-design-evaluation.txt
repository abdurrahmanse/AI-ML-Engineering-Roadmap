Level 8 · Experiments & Ablations

Phase: Architectures & Experiments
Suggested duration: 28 sessions (~7 weeks)
Prerequisites: Level 7
Scope: Master the scientific method in machine learning: design controlled experiments, enforce baseline compute parity, conduct definitive component ablations, and report statistical significance for top-tier venues.

M8.1 · Scientific Methodology, Variables & Factorial Experimental Design — (sessions: 5)
Session 1 · Principles of scientific control in AI research
- Variable Identification: Isolate independent variables (model components, hyperparameters), dependent variables (task metrics, efficiency), and control nuisance variables.
- Experimental Units & Randomization: Define the unit of analysis, enforce random assignment of seeds, and prevent confounding through stratified block designs.
Session 2 · Factorial designs & interaction effects
- OFAT vs Factorial Design: Contrast One-Factor-At-A-Time (OFAT) screening with 2^k Full Factorial Designs to measure non-linear interaction effects between hyperparameters.
- Fractional Factorial Designs: Apply orthogonal fractional factorial designs to screen high-dimensional hyperparameter spaces under strict compute budgets.
Mental model: An experiment without rigorous controls is merely an observation; scientific claims require proving that observed improvements stem strictly from the isolated intervention.
Try: Design a 2^3 factorial experiment testing learning rate, batch size, and weight decay on an image classification task, computing main effects and two-way interaction terms.
Outcome: You can design factorial experiments, isolate confounding factors, and quantify interaction effects between architectural components.

M8.2 · Fair Baselines, Compute Parity & Hyperparameter Optimization — (sessions: 5)
Session 1 · The baseline fairness crisis in AI literature
- The Under-Tuned Baseline Trap: Audit published literature for biased comparisons where a proposed method is heavily tuned while baselines receive default hyperparameters.
- Compute Budget Parity: Equalize total computational resources (FLOPs, wall-clock hours, or hyperparameter trial counts) across all competing baseline models.
Session 2 · Principled hyperparameter search protocols
- Search Algorithms: Compare Random Search against Bayesian Optimization using Gaussian Processes and Tree-structured Parzen Estimators (TPE via Optuna).
- Nested Cross-Validation: Enforce nested cross-validation (outer loop for model assessment, inner loop for hyperparameter tuning) to prevent test-set over-fitting.
Mental model: A novel method has not proven superiority if an established baseline, given equal tuning compute and modern training recipes, matches its performance.
Try: Benchmark a novel attention variant against a tuned standard baseline where both models receive an identical 50-trial Optuna tuning budget, comparing final test distributions.
Outcome: You can design fair benchmark comparisons under strict compute parity and tune baselines using Bayesian optimization without test leakage.

M8.3 · Ablation Study Design & Component Isolation — (sessions: 5)
Session 1 · Destructive vs constructive ablation protocols
- Destructive Ablations: Remove components one by one from the full proposed system to measure the unique performance degradation attributable to each isolated mechanism.
- Constructive Ablations: Add proposed components step-by-step onto the minimal baseline to verify cumulative, monotonic improvements.
Session 2 · Multi-dimensional ablation axes
- Architectural Ablations: Measure impact of removing skip connections, varying layer counts, disabling attention heads, and swapping normalization layers.
- Objective & Loss Ablations: Evaluate contribution of auxiliary losses, contrastive penalties, and loss weighting coefficients (lambda_1, lambda_2).
- Data Scaling Ablations: Evaluate performance curves across training data subsamples (1%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 100%) to test sample efficiency claims.
Mental model: An ablation study is an autopsy of your model that proves which specific parts of your proposed method actually drive the claimed empirical gains.
Try: Execute a 6-row ablation table on a sequence model systematically removing positional embeddings, layer normalization, and warmup, reporting exact delta metrics.
Outcome: You can design, execute, and report conclusive destructive and constructive ablation studies that isolate genuine scientific contributions.

M8.4 · Statistical Hypothesis Testing in Benchmarks & Error Analysis — (sessions: 5)
Session 1 · Multi-seed replication & non-parametric significance
- Seed Variance Protocols: Execute every experimental condition across N >= 5 random seeds to report mean and standard deviation (mu +/- sigma).
- Non-Parametric Significance: Compute the paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test and bootstrap 95% confidence intervals, evaluating whether differences are statistically distinguishable.
Session 2 · Fine-grained error slicing & failure taxonomies
- Subgroup Slicing: Stratify test errors across input length, class imbalance, entity frequency, and demographic subgroups to detect hidden performance collapse.
- Failure Mode Taxonomy: Categorize model errors into explicit failure modes (e.g. hallucination, boundary error, catastrophic forgetting) and analyze representative edge cases.
Mental model: Aggregate benchmark scores hide critical failure modes; rigorous science requires inspecting error distributions across subgroups and random seeds.
Try: Construct an automated error-slicing dashboard that categorizes model errors across difficulty deciles and computes bootstrap confidence intervals for subgroup accuracy.
Outcome: You can execute multi-seed statistical significance tests, perform deep error taxonomies, and report robust confidence intervals.

M8.5 · Composing the Empirical Section of an Academic Paper — (sessions: 4)
Session 1 · Writing experimental setup & baseline descriptions
- Experimental Setup Writing: Draft reproducible setup sections documenting datasets, preprocessing, baseline hyperparameter grids, and hardware environments.
- Metric Justification: Defend the choice of evaluation metrics against target real-world objectives, acknowledging metric limitations.
Session 2 · Designing publication-grade tables & Pareto plots
- Scientific Table Formatting: Construct publication-grade LaTeX booktabs tables highlighting statistically significant best results, including standard deviations and sample sizes.
- Scientific Data Visualization: Plot Pareto frontiers (accuracy vs latency/FLOPs), violin plots over random seeds, and ablation contribution bar charts with error bars.
Mental model: The empirical section of a paper is a scientific courtroom; your tables and figures are the evidence presented to skeptical reviewers.
Try: Generate a publication-ready LaTeX results table and a matplotlib Pareto frontier plot comparing accuracy versus parameter count across 5 models.
Outcome: You can author rigorous experimental setup sections and create publication-grade tables and Pareto frontier visualizations.

M8.6 · Capstone: Publication-Grade Empirical Benchmark & Ablation Study Suite — (sessions: 4)
Session 1 · Comprehensive benchmark execution under compute parity
- Select an open research challenge, implement a proposed method, and benchmark it against three strong, equally tuned baselines under compute parity across 5 random seeds.
- Execute a full 5-part ablation matrix isolating architectural choices, loss function terms, and training data subsamples.
Session 2 · Manuscript empirical section & open-source package
- Author a 6-page empirical manuscript section in LaTeX containing Experimental Setup, Main Results Table with Wilcoxon p-values, Ablation Matrix, and Error Analysis.
- Package the entire experimental harness with automated seed execution scripts, raw log files, and interactive visualization notebooks.
Final result: A fully verified, publication-grade benchmark and ablation study suite complete with non-parametric statistical significance testing and a LaTeX manuscript draft.
